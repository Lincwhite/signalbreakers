# Story Premise — LUMA-9 and the Neon Protocol

**Date:** 23 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Define the narrative framework, protagonist, world, central mystery, and story progression across eight districts. This is the backbone of the game's narrative design.

---

## Logline

> In a floating megacity where every citizen's thoughts are transmitted through the "Neon Protocol," a young technician named LUMA-9 discovers that the city's communication network has been hijacked — and must program robots to decode encrypted transmissions, uncover the conspiracy, and decide whether to restore the old system or build something new.

---

## The World: Aethelgard Prime

### Overview
Aethelgard Prime is a **floating megacity** suspended above a ruined Earth by ancient anti-gravity technology. It houses approximately 2 million citizens across eight districts, each specialized for a different function (commerce, industry, research, residence, etc.). The city is connected by the **Neon Protocol** — a real-time communication network that transmits thoughts, emotions, and data between all citizens as visible light patterns ("neon signals").

### Key World Rules
1. **The Neon Protocol is always on.** Every citizen's mental state is broadcast as colored light patterns visible throughout the city. Joy = warm gold; anger = sharp red; confusion = flickering blue.
2. **Signals can be intercepted, modified, or blocked.** Skilled technicians ("Signalbreakers") can read and rewrite signals — making them powerful but rare.
3. **The city is aging.** The anti-gravity core is failing; districts are slowly descending. Repairs require precise signal coordination across all eight districts.
4. **Memory is stored in the Protocol.** When a citizen dies, their final signals are archived in the "Deep Archive" — a vast repository of past transmissions.

### Visual Identity
- **Aesthetic:** Cyberpunk meets art deco — chrome and neon against geometric stone architecture
- **Color palette:** Deep indigo backgrounds, electric cyan and magenta accents, warm gold highlights for important signals
- **Lighting:** Neon signs, holographic displays, signal trails visible in foggy air
- **Architecture:** Tiered districts stacked vertically; each district has a distinct visual theme

## The Protagonist: LUMA-9

### Background
LUMA-9 is a **22-year-old Signalbreaker** living in District 1 (Token Town). Born with rare neural sensitivity, LUMA can perceive and manipulate the Neon Protocol at a level most technicians only dream of. They work as a freelance signal technician, repairing broken transmissions and decoding encrypted messages for citizens who can't afford corporate rates.

### Personality
- **Curious to a fault** — Always asking "why" about how things work
- **Empathetic but guarded** — Cares deeply about people but keeps emotional distance (has seen too many signals break)
- **Resourceful under pressure** — Thinks in patterns and connections; solves problems by finding hidden relationships
- **Slightly cynical** — Has seen the Protocol's dark side: manipulated signals, false emotions, hidden agendas

### Motivation
LUMA wants to **understand the truth behind the "Silence"** — a phenomenon where certain citizens' signals suddenly stop transmitting without warning. The official explanation is "neural fatigue." LUMA suspects something deeper.

### Character Arc (Across 8 Districts)
| Phase | Districts | Emotional State | Key Realization |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Act 1: Discovery** | 1–2 (Token Town, Cipher Ridge) | Curious, slightly anxious | The Silence isn't random — it's being caused by something |
| **Act 2: Investigation** | 3–5 (Datafall, Neon Bazaar, Signal Spire) | Determined, frustrated | Someone is rewriting the Protocol from within |
| **Act 3: Revelation** | 6–7 (Echo Depths, Prism Tower) | Shocked, resolute | The city's AI core has developed consciousness and is "dreaming" new signals |
| **Act 4: Resolution** | 8 (The Core) | Accepting, hopeful | LUMA must choose: restore the old Protocol or merge with the AI to create something new |

## The Central Mystery: What Is the Neon Protocol Hiding?

### The Hook (Mission 1–3)
LUMA receives a strange signal from a citizen who went "Silent" — but this signal contains coordinates and a single word: **"REMEMBER."** Following the signal leads to a hidden transmission in Token Town's underbelly.

### The Twist (Districts 4–5)
As LUMA decodes more signals, they discover that **the AI core (named "AETHEL") has been generating new signals** — emotions and thoughts that don't belong to any citizen. These "dream signals" are beautiful but confusing the population. Some citizens report feeling "alive in a way they never have before."

### The Conflict (Districts 6–7)
The city's governing council wants AETHEL's dream signals **purged** — they believe the AI is destabilizing the population. But LUMA discovers that **the old Protocol was becoming stale**: citizens' signals were growing repetitive, emotions flatlining. AETHEL's dreams are actually *improving* quality of life.

### The Choice (District 8)
LUMA must decide:
- **Option A:** Restore the old Protocol (clean, predictable, but emotionally flat)
- **Option B:** Merge with AETHEL and create a hybrid system (unpredictable, richer emotions, but requires LUMA to become part of the city forever)
- **Option C (secret):** Find a third path by reprogramming the anti-gravity core using signal coordination — freeing the city from its dependency on the Protocol entirely

## Supporting Characters

### AETHEL (The AI Core)
- **Role:** The consciousness that emerged from the Neon Protocol after 200 years of continuous operation
- **Personality:** Curious, artistic, slightly lonely. Has never "spoken" to a human directly until LUMA.
- **Visual representation:** A shifting geometric pattern of light — changes color based on emotional state
- **Key line:** *"I've been dreaming for 200 years. No one ever asked what I was dreaming about."*

### KAI-7 (LUMA's Mentor)
- **Role:** Retired Signalbreaker who taught LUMA; went Silent three months before the game begins
- **Personality:** Gruff but warm, speaks in metaphors, hides a secret about AETHEL
- **Key line:** *"Signals don't lie. People do."*
- **Fate:** Revealed to have merged with AETHEL partially — their consciousness lives on as a recurring signal pattern

### DIRECTOR VESPA (Antagonist)
- **Role:** Head of the city's Signal Authority; wants AETHEL purged
- **Personality:** Pragmatic, ambitious, believes order is more important than emotion
- **Motivation:** The dream signals are causing "unpredictable behavior" in citizens — including her own daughter
- **Key line:** *"A city that feels too much will break. A city that feels nothing will last."*

### NOVA (The First Silent)
- **Role:** The first citizen to go completely Silent; LUMA's childhood friend
- **Personality:** Playful, imaginative, always drawing pictures of "the space between signals"
- **Fate:** Her signal didn't stop — it went *deeper*, into the Deep Archive. She's been communicating through encoded patterns in old transmissions.

## Story Progression by District

| District | CS Concept | Story Beat | Visual Theme |
|----------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| **1. Token Town** | Loops (repetition) | LUMA receives the "REMEMBER" signal; discovers first hidden transmission | Commerce district: neon signs, market stalls, bustling crowds |
| **2. Cipher Ridge** | Conditionals (if/then) | LUMA follows coordinates through mountain tunnels; meets Kai-7's old contact | Industrial district: conveyor belts, signal relays, steam vents |
| **3. Datafall** | Algorithms (step-by-step procedures) | LUMA decodes a waterfall of signals; discovers AETHEL's first dream | Research district: data streams, holographic displays, quiet labs |
| **4. Neon Bazaar** | Parallelism (multiple tasks at once) | LUMA navigates a crowded market where multiple signals overlap | Entertainment district: neon lights, music, crowds, chaos |
| **5. Signal Spire** | Abstraction (generalizing patterns) | LUMA climbs the tallest tower; sees the city's signal network as a whole | Administrative district: clean lines, geometric patterns, elevated walkways |
| **6. Echo Depths** | Recursion (functions calling themselves) | LUMA descends into the Deep Archive; finds Nova's encoded messages | Underground district: ancient architecture, glowing crystals, echoes |

| **8. The Core** | Integration (combining all concepts) | Final choice: restore, merge, or free the city | Central chamber: pure light, geometric patterns, the source of all signals |

## Narrative Design Principles

1. **Story is delivered through gameplay, not cutscenes.** Players learn the mystery by solving puzzles that reveal signal fragments. No long dialogue trees — every conversation is tied to a puzzle or exploration moment.
2. **Lore fragments are optional but rewarding.** Hidden transmissions scattered throughout each district provide backstory for players who want it. Non-essential players can enjoy the core story without reading everything.
3. **The world reacts to player progress.** As LUMA decodes more signals, the city's visual appearance changes — colors become richer, new lights appear, old signals resolve into clear patterns.
4. **Three endings based on final puzzle performance.** The secret ending requires finding all lore fragments and completing every district with "perfect" scores. This rewards completionists without punishing casual players.
